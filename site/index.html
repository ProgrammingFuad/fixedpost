SEC. 01

One platform.
The entire program.

Every operational requirement of a paid detail program — scheduling through 1099 — handled in a single system of record. No spreadsheets, no side channels, no gaps in the audit trail.

01 / SCHEDULING

Automated Scheduling

Clients post details; eligible officers see and claim them in seconds. Business rules, eligibility checks, and validation run on every action.

  • Rules engine for UMOS eligibility criteria
  • Auto un-scheduling of ineligible officers
  • Service Eligibility Flag enforcement
  • Automated notifications on every change
02 / ATTENDANCE

Digital Check-In / Out

Secure mobile attendance verification with electronic paid detail slips — generated, signed, and printable across mobile and desktop.

  • Mobile check-in/check-out verification
  • Electronic detail slips, print-ready
  • PDU notifications on officer check-in
  • Offline mode for mission-critical features
03 / BILLING

Consolidated Invoicing

One itemized invoice per assignment — officer compensation, the 10% administrative fee, and the platform fee — collected once, distributed automatically.

  • ACH, wire, and major credit cards
  • PCI-DSS compliant, end-to-end encrypted
  • Automated dunning for outstanding balances
  • Pre-pay and post-pay client models
04 / PAYOUTS

Guaranteed Officer Pay

Direct deposit to every officer no later than Friday of the week following the detail — funded by us even when a client pays late.

  • $0 in fees to officers, ever
  • W-9 upload, review & PDU approval flow
  • Consolidated 1099 per officer by Feb 1
  • Full transaction history & audit trail
05 / OVERSIGHT

PDU Command & Control

Real-time visibility into assignment status, attendance, payments, and performance — with supervised override authority when the job demands it.

  • Cap override, restricted to PDU supervisors
  • Every override logged: user, time, rationale
  • Agency coordinator verification views
  • Client vetting, exclusion & restriction tools
06 / COMMUNICATION

Secure Messaging

Real-time, in-platform communication between officers and clients, with group messaging and department-wide announcements.

  • Officer ↔ client secure channels
  • Group messaging & broadcast announcements
  • Guidelines acknowledgment gate at enrollment
  • Automated shift reminders, both sides
